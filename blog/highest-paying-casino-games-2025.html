🎲 Highest Paying Table Games

Blackjack (99.5%+ RTP with Optimal Strategy)

When played with perfect basic strategy, blackjack offers the best odds in the casino—often exceeding 99.5% RTP. Single-deck blackjack with favorable rules can reach 99.8% RTP.

Key Rules for Best RTP:

  • Single or double deck (not 6-8 decks)
  • Dealer stands on soft 17
  • Double after split allowed
  • Late surrender available
  • Blackjack pays 3:2 (not 6:5)

⚠️ Common Misconceptions About RTP

Myth: Higher RTP = Guaranteed Wins

False. RTP is calculated over millions of spins. Individual sessions are determined by variance and luck.

Myth: RTP Changes During Your Session

False. RTP is fixed for each game. Casinos can't adjust it mid-session (that would be illegal).

Myth: Table Games Always Beat Slots

Not Always. Football Plinko (99% RTP) beats most table games. Top slots rival blackjack.

Myth: "Hot" and "Cold" Games

False. Past results don't influence future spins. Every spin is independent with the same RTP.
