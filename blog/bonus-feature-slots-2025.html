🎯 Types of Bonus Features Explained

🎰 Free Spins

The most common bonus. Land 3+ scatter symbols to trigger a set number of free spins, often with enhanced features like multipliers or sticky wilds.

🔄 Respins

After a winning combination, certain symbols stick and remaining positions respin. Can trigger multiple times for escalating wins.

📈 Multipliers

Increase winning payouts by 2x, 3x, 5x or more. Can be fixed or progressive, sometimes combining for massive multipliers.

📌 Sticky Symbols

Special symbols remain in position for multiple spins, increasing winning potential. Often combined with respins.

🎁 Bonus Buy

Purchase direct access to bonus rounds for a set price (typically 50-100x bet). Skip base game and jump straight to features.

💰 Pick-and-Win

Interactive bonus where you select objects to reveal prizes, multipliers, or more picks.

🎲 Cascading Reels

Winning symbols disappear and new symbols drop down, potentially creating multiple consecutive wins from one spin.

💡 Maximizing Bonus Feature Potential

1. Understand Trigger Mechanics

Learn how bonuses activate. Most require 3+ scatters, but some have alternative triggers. Read the paytable!

2. Bankroll for Bonus Hunting

Bonuses can take 100-200 spins to trigger. Ensure your bankroll can sustain the wait.

3. Evaluate Bonus Buy Value

Bonus Buy can be worthwhile if:

  • You have limited playtime
  • The bonus offers high RTP
  • The cost is reasonable (50-75x typical bet)
  • Your bankroll supports it

4. Track Bonus Frequency

Some slots trigger bonuses frequently (every 50-100 spins), others rarely (200-300 spins). Choose based on your patience level.

5. Focus During Bonuses

Bonus rounds determine most of your wins. Pay attention to mechanics, don't autopilot through them.
